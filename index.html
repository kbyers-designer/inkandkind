THE HAND BEHIND THE INK

Field experience, drawn into every line.

Artist and wildlife rehabilitator Katie Byers makes work rooted in more than thirty years of hands-on animal care on the Texas Gulf Coast. The subjects are often scrappy, misunderstood, or too useful to be glamorous.

That is exactly the point. The overlooked ones deserve good art, too.
